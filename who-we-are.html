About

Seeing what conventional supply chain maps miss.

The Observatory connects academic research, business experience and student work to examine how geopolitical change moves through supply chains and reaches companies, industries and regions.

Founder and lead researcher

Dr. Cristiane Biazzin

I am an associate professor of Supply Chain Management and Operations at the Haile College of Business, Northern Kentucky University, where I have taught since 2019. Before that, I spent eight years as a professor at FGV in Brazil, from 2011 to 2019. Before entering academia, I spent more than two decades in operations and supply chain roles for Latin America at multinational enterprises, experiences that continue to shape the questions I ask and the way I see the world.

Throughout my career, first in industry and later in academia, I encountered questions that initially seemed unrelated. Some emerged inside manufacturing plants; others surfaced during sourcing decisions, international projects, or research on economic development. Over time, they began to point in the same direction: Why are experienced companies still surprised by events that appear far removed from their daily operations? Why do important vulnerabilities remain hidden for years, only becoming visible when something goes wrong?

I study supply chains from a socio-political and geopolitical perspective. My work began in operations and supply chain management and now examines strategic dependencies, geopolitical tensions, and the growing role of digital and space-enabled infrastructure in everyday operations.
