Dr. Cristiane Biazzin

Founder and Lead Researcher

I am an associate professor of Supply Chain Management and Operations at Northern Kentucky University, a researcher, and a former industry practitioner. Before entering academia, I spent more than two decades working with manufacturing and supply chain operations, experiences that continue to shape the questions I ask and the way I see the world.

Throughout my career, first in industry and later in academia, I accumulated questions that seemed unrelated at first. Some emerged inside manufacturing plants, while others surfaced during sourcing decisions, international projects, or research on economic development. Over time, I began to realize that they were all pointing in the same direction: Why are experienced companies still surprised by events that appear far removed from their daily operations? Why do important vulnerabilities remain hidden for years, only becoming visible when something goes wrong?

My academic journey began with a focus on operations and supply chain management. Gradually, that focus expanded to the coevolution of global production networks and emerging economies, and recently to strategic dependencies, geopolitical tensions, and the growing role of digital and space-enabled infrastructures in supporting everyday operations.
