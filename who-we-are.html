Dr. Cristiane Biazzin

Founder and Lead Researcher

I am an associate professor of Supply Chain Management and Operations at Northern Kentucky University, a researcher, and a former industry practitioner. Before entering academia, I spent more than two decades working with manufacturing and supply chain operations, experiences that continue to shape the questions I ask and the way I see the world.

Throughout my career, first in industry and later in academia, I encountered questions that initially seemed unrelated. Some emerged inside manufacturing plants; others surfaced during sourcing decisions, international projects, or research on economic development. Over time, they began to point in the same direction: Why are experienced companies still surprised by events that appear far removed from their daily operations? Why do important vulnerabilities remain hidden for years, only becoming visible when something goes wrong?

My academic work began in operations and supply chain management and expanded to the coevolution of global production networks and emerging economies. It now examines strategic dependencies, geopolitical tensions, and the growing role of digital and space-enabled infrastructure in everyday operations.
