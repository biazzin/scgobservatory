Join the Observatory

Good research often begins with a question someone cannot ignore.

The Observatory connects organizations, students, and researchers around consequential questions about geopolitics and supply chains.

For Organizations

Need to understand your own company’s exposure to tariffs, sourcing shifts, or geopolitical change? Start with an Exposure Readout and see how we work with companies.

Work with the Observatory ›

Organizations can also support the Observatory’s mission by sponsoring research projects, funding student scholarships and conference participation, and partnering with us on events.

For Students

Interested in going beyond the classroom? The Observatory offers opportunities to work with real questions, investigate evidence, develop research skills, and strengthen critical thinking. Students can contribute to ongoing projects, develop their own research questions, and learn how to move from curiosity to evidence-based analysis.

For Researchers

Research questions rarely fit neatly within disciplines or borders. We welcome researchers interested in developing collaborative studies on supply chain geopolitics, strategic dependencies, global production networks, institutional change, and related questions. Wherever you are based, we would be interested in hearing from you.
