The central finding

The effective duty rate more than tripled in one year.

Import value increased 3.1%, from $133.28 billion to $137.43 billion. Estimated calculated duties increased 232.4%, from $3.83 billion to $12.74 billion. The effective duty rate rose from 2.88% to 9.27%.

This divergence is consistent with the changing U.S. tariff environment in 2025. District-level data cannot determine how much of the increase came from specific tariff measures or changes in the mix of countries and products.

Calculated Duties are Census estimates and do not necessarily equal amounts ultimately paid. The tariff analysis covers the Cleveland Customs District, District 41, a broad customs geography that includes Cleveland, Cincinnati, Columbus, Dayton, Toledo–Sandusky, Indianapolis, Louisville and additional ports. It also includes dedicated Schedule D codes for UPS Courier, DHL Cincinnati and FedEx Indianapolis. CVG is home to Amazon Air’s primary U.S. hub, DHL’s Global Super Hub for the Americas and a significant FedEx presence, but district-level results cannot isolate CVG or the activity of individual carriers. Sources: U.S. Census trade definitions, Schedule D district and port codes, and CVG Air Cargo.
