About the data

A relationship-level view of regional trade.

Values aggregate all reported Harmonized System product categories for the Cincinnati–Lawrenceburg, OH port district. A zero indicates that the source file reported no trade for that country–product–year combination. The chart describes trade recorded through the port district; it does not measure the complete economy of the Greater Cincinnati metropolitan area.

Source: U.S. Census Bureau, USA Trade Online. Data for 2017 and 2025.
