Exposure Readout

45 minutes

Where should we look first?

You share your main import categories and source countries. We read them against the Observatory's data and tell you, in plain terms, where your exposure is likely concentrated.

  • Your top three exposure points
  • A one-page summary for your leadership
  • A recommendation on whether to go deeper

Exposure Map

3 weeks

How exposed are we, product by product?

A product-level study of your import base: duty exposure, country and supplier concentration, alternative origins already gaining ground, and what each scenario does to your costs.

  • Exposure map by product and origin
  • Cost impact under three trade scenarios
  • Prioritized actions with decision triggers
  • Executive briefing for your leadership team

Scenario Room

Half day

What would we actually do if it happened tomorrow?

Your leadership team works through realistic disruptions built on your own suppliers and products: a new tariff, the loss of a critical source, a closed route. Decisions get made in the room, not later.

  • A session designed around your supply base
  • Documented decisions and trade-offs
  • A contingency plan with clear owners

Signal Briefing

Quarterly

What changed this quarter that matters to us?

A private briefing for your executives or board on the trade, tariff and sourcing shifts that touch your categories, before they show up in your costs.

  • A quarterly briefing tailored to your categories
  • Early signals with what to watch next
  • Direct access between briefings

Trace · Measure · Decide

1–3 days, in company

Can your team see the risks no supplier map shows?

Our own research method, taught on your products, suppliers and markets. Your team follows a product past its direct suppliers, quantifies the exposure with public trade and tariff data, and sets the triggers that tell them when to act.

  • Optional modules: triad thinking beyond U.S.–China, tariff-smart supplier negotiation, invisible infrastructure risk
