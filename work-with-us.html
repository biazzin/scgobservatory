Work With Us

Know your exposure before it shows up in your costs.

We analyze your exposure for you, or we train your team to do it themselves. Every engagement has a fixed scope and a clear output.

Track 1 · We analyze for you

Evidence on your company’s exposure, delivered to your leadership.

SCG-0145 minutes · Starting point

Exposure Readout

Where should we look first?

You share your main import categories and source countries. We read them against the Observatory’s data and tell you, in plain terms, where your exposure is likely concentrated.

You leave with

  • Your top three exposure points
  • A one-page summary for your leadership
  • A recommendation on whether to go deeper
SCG-023 weeks · Diagnostic

Exposure Map

How exposed are we, product by product?

A product-level study of your import base: duty exposure, country and supplier concentration, alternative origins already gaining ground, and what each scenario does to your costs.

You receive

  • Exposure map by product and origin
  • Cost impact under three trade scenarios
  • Prioritized actions with decision triggers
  • Executive briefing for your leadership team
SCG-03Half day · Leadership session

Scenario Room

What would we actually do if it happened tomorrow?

Your leadership team works through realistic disruptions built on your own suppliers and products: a new tariff, the loss of a critical source, a closed route. Decisions get made in the room, not later.

You receive

  • A session designed around your supply base
  • Documented decisions and trade-offs
  • A contingency plan with clear owners
SCG-04Quarterly · Ongoing

Signal Briefing

What changed this quarter that matters to us?

A private briefing for your executives or board on the trade, tariff and sourcing shifts that touch your categories, before they show up in your costs.

You receive

  • A quarterly briefing tailored to your categories
  • Early signals with what to watch next
  • Direct access between briefings

Track 2 · We build your team’s capability

Instead of depending on us, your people learn to read global shifts and work with trade data themselves.

SCG-05Signature program · 1–3 days · In company

Trace · Measure · Decide

Can your team see the risks no supplier map shows?

Built on the Observatory’s own research method and tailored to your industry. Your team works on your products, your suppliers and your markets, and leaves able to repeat the process without us.

  1. TraceFollow one of your own products beyond your direct suppliers to the geopolitical chokepoints that shape its cost.
  2. MeasureQuantify the exposure with public trade and tariff data, by product and by country of origin.
  3. DecideTurn signals into decision triggers, so your team knows in advance when to act and when to wait.

Optional modules

  • Beyond U.S.–China: triad thinking
  • Tariff-smart supplier negotiation
  • Invisible infrastructure risk

Speaking

What should our people understand about the global shifts ahead?

Keynotes and panels for conferences, industry associations and leadership meetings, built on current Observatory data and tailored to your audience.

Topics include

  • Tariffs and the new cost of sourcing
  • Where nearshoring is really going
  • Hidden dependencies in global supply chains
SCG-07Hands-on · Team workshop

Trade Analytics Lab

How do we build this analysis ourselves?

A working session where your team learns to find, analyze and interpret public trade and tariff data for your own products, and leaves with a model they keep using.

Your team leaves with

  • Skills to work with Census and tariff data
  • An exposure model built on your products
  • A routine to monitor changes every quarter
