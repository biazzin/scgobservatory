Flagship program · Geopolitical triads

Beyond Bilateral Exposure

This study examines how economic tensions between the United States and China are reshaping supply chains involving Brazil. It uses data analytics and company interviews to identify which products and industries are changing and how companies are responding.

It also asks whether Brazil is building a more strategic production role or becoming an intermediary exposed to greater geopolitical and trade risk.
