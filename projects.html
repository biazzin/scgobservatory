Our Work

Understanding how global change becomes a business consequence.

We connect long-term questions with focused investigations, regional evidence, and conversations that bring research into practice.

What we investigate

Four connected areas of inquiry

01

Geopolitical triads and supply chain reconfiguration

How interventions between two countries create redirection, opportunity, exposure, and strategic change in third countries.

02

Strategic dependencies and resilience

How organizations understand and sometimes overlook the shared infrastructure and enabling systems behind operational continuity.

03

Trade policy and industrial transformation

How tariffs, sanctions, investment restrictions, and industrial policy influence firm decisions and regional competitiveness.

04

Signals, narratives, and managerial judgment

How business leaders interpret incomplete geopolitical signals and translate uncertainty into sourcing, investment, and operational choices.

How we work

Evidence guides the question.

Our projects combine product-level trade data, archival evidence, interviews, field investigation, company and industry cases, and collaboration across disciplines. The method follows the question and the strength of the available evidence.
